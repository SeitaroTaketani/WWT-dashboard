Sources: UN Comtrade (16 HS 6-digit water and wastewater treatment-related goods; importer-reported values, exporter-reported mirror values where importers do not report); WITS/TRAINS simple-average tariffs, latest available year; WHO/UNICEF JMP 2024 (population without safely managed drinking-water services).

Note: Ground colour shows water-access need; wall height and colour (3D) or circle colour (2D) show the tariff (capped at 20%); circles show estimated duties paid by each importer (trade value × importer's tariff), coloured by the effective duty rate; arcs (on hover, or via "Show") run from supplier to importer and widen towards the importer. Many of the HS codes are also used outside water treatment. Symbolic rings mark small economies whose borders are too small to draw.
